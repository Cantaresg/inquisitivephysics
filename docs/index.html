The Approach

Built on a simple idea.

Students don't remember what they're told. They remember what surprised them. Every module here is designed to create that surprise.

01
🤔
You make a prediction first.

Before any experiment runs, you commit to what you think will happen. This creates the conditions for genuine surprise — and genuine learning.

02
⚗️
The experiment runs.

Physics-accurate simulations using real equations — not cartoons. What you see is what would actually happen in a lab, without the setup time.

03
💡
You explain what you saw.

Observation questions push you to articulate the physics in your own words. The sim doesn't tell you the answer — you construct it yourself.

04
📊
Data tells the deeper story.

Collect measurements, spot patterns, graph relationships. The same method Galileo used 400 years ago — and still how science works today.

"Tell me and I forget. Teach me and I remember. Involve me and I learn."

Benjamin Franklin · The principle behind every simulation here
